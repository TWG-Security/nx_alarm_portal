Alarm levels

Critical

A pop-up on every page and a siren repeating every 4 seconds, until acknowledged or silenced.

Alarm

A red card in the live feed and a chime repeating every 30 seconds, until acknowledged or silenced.

Warning

An amber card in the live feed and one soft tone when it arrives.

Ignore

Not stored or shown. The event still appears in NX.
