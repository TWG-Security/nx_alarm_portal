Alarm alert text messages: terms and privacy

TWG Security Alarm Portal · Last updated {{ updated }}

The program

TWG Security Alarm Portal alarm alerts are text messages sent by TWG Security (The Wire Guys, twgsecurity.com) to staff and authorized contacts of the organizations whose security systems TWG Security monitors. Each message reports one security alarm (what happened, the site, the camera and the time) with a link to view that alarm. They are operational alerts, never marketing.

How you sign up (opt in)

  1. An administrator of your organization adds your mobile number to your user account in the TWG Security Alarm Portal and chooses which alarms you should receive.
  2. You receive one text{% if sender %} from {{ sender }}{% endif %}: "TWG Security Alarm Portal: … added this number to get alarm alerts for … Reply YES to sign up. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out at any time. Terms: {{ portal }}/sms-terms"
  3. Nothing else is sent unless you reply YES. When you do, you get a confirmation text and alarm alerts start.

Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Your reply and its date are recorded.

How you stop (opt out)

Reply STOP to any message at any time (STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT work too). You get one confirmation and no further messages. To sign up again, reply START or YES. You can also ask your organization's administrator, or TWG Security, to remove your number.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact TWG Security: {{ contact }}.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies: you receive a text for each alarm at the sites and levels your organization chose for you, which can be several a day or none for weeks. Flood protection limits how many arrive in a short time. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan.

Delivery

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Text alerts are a convenience on top of the portal's monitoring screens, not a replacement for them: don't rely on a text alone for life-safety decisions.

Alarm links

Each alert includes a private link that opens that one alarm (details, recorded clip and live camera) without signing in, for a limited time. Anyone with the link can see that alarm, so please don't forward it.

Privacy

  • Your mobile number is used only to send these alarm alerts and to handle your replies (YES, STOP, HELP).
  • Mobile numbers and text-message consent are not sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. They are shared only with the text-message provider (Twilio) that delivers the messages, and with mobile carriers as needed to deliver them.
  • We keep a record of each message sent (number, time, result) and of your opt-in and opt-out, for compliance and support. Message links are not stored.
  • To have your number removed, reply STOP or contact TWG Security: {{ contact }}.
